# Fixed system vocabularies

`tang_decoder_vocab.json` contains the 6,867-word decoder vocabulary used by
Tang et al. (2023) for semantic reconstruction from fMRI. The paper defines it
as the unique words occurring at least twice in the encoding-model training
data (doi:10.1038/s41593-023-01304-9).

`meg_masc_2023_v50.csv` is generated by `scripts/build_systems_csv.py` from
the four pre-tokenized MEG-MASC stimulus stories supplied with this analysis.
The builder pools whitespace tokens across the files, applies NFKC
normalisation and case-folding, excludes standalone punctuation, preserves
clitic tokens such as `'s` and `n't`, sorts by decreasing count, and breaks
ties alphabetically. The CSV records the exact rank, decoder label, and pooled
count used by the contour, main-table, and reference-dependence pipelines.
